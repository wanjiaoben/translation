About government support programs

The Japanese Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare's revised October 1, 2026 employment-environment program references multilingual versions of work rules or collective agreements, employment terms notices or contracts, and internal manuals.

The grant is for eligible employers, not a guarantee attached to purchasing our translation services. Employers must check their approved plans, requirements and eligible expenses. We do not act as a grant application representative.

Frequently asked questions

Will hiring your translation service guarantee a government subsidy?

No. Japan's support grants are generally assessed for the employer under eligibility and approved-plan conditions. Our translation services do not guarantee qualification, approval or payment.

Can all 13 options be delivered immediately?

We accept inquiries across these 13 language and script options. We confirm suitable translators, specialist review, timing, and price before accepting each assignment.

Do you offer legal or social-insurance labor consultant services?

We offer translation, interpretation and coordination. Legal drafting, compliance judgments and grant application representation should be performed by appropriately licensed professionals.
